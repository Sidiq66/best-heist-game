**Gilt & Gone** — an original top-down stealth heist game in plain HTML5 canvas and JavaScript, no build step and no network. Sneak through three vaults of the Meridian Trust — the Lobby, the Gallery and the deep Vault — reading the guards' patrols and vision cones and the cameras' sweeps, timing the laser grids, and hiding behind the glass display cases. Grab the named prize (the Aurelia Star, the Sultana Emerald, the Orlov Moon) plus every stack of cash and gold bar you dare, then get out before the countdown seals the vault. Being seen feeds the alarm meter; at 100% the vault locks down. Three tools help — an EMP pulse that darkens nearby cameras and lasers, a smoke bomb that hides you, and a tossed coin that lures a guard — and every one of them is deducted from your take, so a clean ghost run pays best.

Controls: WASD / arrows to move, Shift to sprint (fast but guards hear it), Ctrl or C to sneak (slow but hard to spot), E for EMP, Q for smoke, click to toss a coin, P to pause, M to mute, R to retry a vault.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```
